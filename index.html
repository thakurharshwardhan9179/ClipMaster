Instagram Reels Setup Link Instagram to your Facebook Page

To publish Instagram Reels directly from ClipMaster, your Instagram account must be a Professional (Business or Creator) account linked to your Facebook Page.

  1. Open your Facebook Page > Settings > Linked Accounts > Instagram.
  2. Click Connect Account and log in to your Instagram Creator/Business account.
  3. When generating your Meta token, ensure instagram_basic & instagram_content_publish permissions are granted.
How to connect Facebook & Instagram (No coding required):
  1. Open Meta's Graph API Explorer ↗
  2. In Permissions dropdown, add:
    pages_show_list pages_manage_posts pages_read_engagement instagram_basic instagram_content_publish
